Research Group


We study the mathematical foundations of data analysis and machine learning, using tools from metric geometry, topology, and probability. A current focus of the group is the geometry of generative models, and in particular how diffusion models generalize.

The group is based in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Missouri. Our work is supported in part by the National Science Foundation through award DMS 2502083 (Mathematical Foundations of Artificial Intelligence).

Principal Investigator

  • Zhengchao Wan, Assistant Professor of Mathematics
