Research Interests

My primary research interests lie in the development of novel mathematical tools and frameworks for understanding and analyzing complex data, with a focus on geometric and topological data analysis. My work spans across multiple domains, including probability theory, geometry, graph theory, and machine learning.

One of my recent focus areas is understanding the generalization of diffusion models from a geometric perspective.
